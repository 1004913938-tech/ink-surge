Arcade ocean racing. Powerslide. Boost. Airtime. Career cups.

## About this game
INK SURGE is a cel-shaded arcade racer set on an infinite procedural ocean. Race a closed buoy circuit against AI, hold a powerslide through the corners to charge a three-tier boost, and take the head-sea straight flat-out for real airtime.

**Modes**
- Career Cup — four events, bronze→platinum medals, unlock boats
- Quick Race — pick any track and go
- Time Trial — solo clock, beat your best
- Garage — Reef Runner / Spine Cutter / Buoy Dancer with liveries

**Tracks (MVP)**
- Novice Bay
- Storm Atoll
- Novice Bay Reverse

## Features
- Procedural Gerstner ocean shared by rendering and buoyancy
- Cel shading with ink outlines
- Career progression with local save
- Keyboard + gamepad

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
